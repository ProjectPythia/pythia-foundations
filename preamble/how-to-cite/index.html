The material in Pythia Foundations is licensed for free and open consumption and reuse. All code is served under Apache 2.0, while all non-code content is licensed under Creative Commons BY 4.0 (CC BY 4.0). Effectively, this means you are free to share and adapt this material so long as you give appropriate credit to the Project Pythia community.

A brief meta-paper describing Pythia Foundations is currently under review at the Journal of Open Source Education. Once a DOI has been issued for this paper, we will post it here as the simplest and recommended way to cite this work.

The source code for the book is released on GitHub and archived on Zenodo. This DOI will always resolve to the latest version of the book source: Rose et al. (2026)

If you wish to cite a specific version of the book, you can use one of the version-specific DOIs from Zenodo, for example Rose et al. (2025).

The full list of Foundations authors and their affiliations is also here on this page! The list grows over time as new individuals make substantial contributions to this ever-evolving work. Join us!
